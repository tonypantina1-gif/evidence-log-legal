# TenantLog Support

Need help with TenantLog? Email [aafdigital@proton.me](mailto:aafdigital@proton.me) and we'll reply within 5 working days. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. There's no need to send your records.

## Common questions

**Where are my records stored?**
On your iPhone. TenantLog has no account and no server of ours. If you turn on iCloud Backup in Settings, a copy is also kept in your own private iCloud, end-to-end encrypted.

**I have a new iPhone. How do I get my records back?**
Turn on iCloud Backup in Settings on your old iPhone before you switch. On the new iPhone, sign in to the same Apple Account, install TenantLog and turn iCloud Backup on there too.

**I bought TenantLog Pro but it isn't unlocked.**
Open Settings in TenantLog and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

**How do I cancel a subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

**How do I share my evidence with my landlord or deposit scheme?**
Create a report from your property and share the PDF by email, AirDrop or Files.

**Can I lock the app?**
Yes. Turn on Face ID or passcode lock in TenantLog's Settings.
